## Record fidelity

This file is an honest prompt record, not a verbatim export of every tool request. The common direction and family-specific inputs below are reconstructed from the approved generation brief; the first six calls were completed earlier and their exact payloads were not recoverable in this workspace. The final three family calls used the same direction with the listed family input. No reconstructed text is labeled as an exact payload.

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. All calls referenced `outputs/card-design/card-families-contact-sheet.png` for the approved tactile card-series direction. The source image is always a portrait front/back pair; no generated words, logo, question, or watermark are used in production. Questions and official branding are rendered by HTML.

## Common prompt
Create one production reference image for the Mingle.Cards card design family. Show a matched vertical front-and-back pair of physical conversation cards on warm cream paper with tactile grain, hand-drawn editorial cut-paper illustration, deep navy ink outlines and a family-specific cheerful palette. The front card has a large quiet cream center and generous blank safe area for real HTML Japanese question text later. The back has a large readable central illustration and a decorative frame. No words, letters, logos, fake typography, question text, devices, UI, or watermark. Both cards fully visible and portrait.
